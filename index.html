User Guide & Instructions:

• LOAD Menu: Click the red LOAD button at the bottom right to upload a local image file, paste directly from clipboard, generate an interactive chromatic color spectrum, or import an image via URL.

• Lens Navigation & Dragging: Click and drag anywhere directly on the image viewport or inside the transparent magnifier lens to smoothly pan across canvas edges (including upper boundaries).

• Zoom Controls: Use your mouse scroll wheel over the lens/zoom slider, adjust the slider input, or click the 100% button to instantly scale up to 30x magnification.

• Color Sampling: Click any pixel location to lock its precise HEX and RGB values into the top readout cards. Click either code card to instantly copy it to your clipboard.

• Handle Toggle: Enable the physical lens handle using the checkbox below to drag by the handle bar instead of the center glass.
